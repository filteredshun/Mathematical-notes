# Perfectoid Siegel Variety

*Notes on Scholze's construction of perfectoid Siegel varieties*

These notes study the geometry and construction of perfectoid Siegel varieties. The **anticanonical tower** is one part of the construction, rather than the title of the entire project.

## Anticanonical Tower

The construction begins near the ordinary locus, using relative Frobenius, the Hasse invariant, and canonical subgroups.
