# Overconvergent Locus

*Extending the anticanonical tower beyond the ordinary locus*

The [[perfectoid-siegel-variety/anticanonical-tower/ordinary-locus|Ordinary Locus]] note constructs the anticanonical tower over the ordinary locus and explains its perfectoid limit.

Here we will study **strict neighborhoods of the ordinary locus** defined using the Hasse invariant, the corresponding canonical subgroup theorems, and the overconvergent anticanonical tower.

The detailed statements and proofs will be added as we work through Scholze §III.2, Zhu §3.9, and Caraiani §4.3.
