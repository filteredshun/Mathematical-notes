# Anticanonical Tower

The anticanonical tower is one part of the construction of the perfectoid Siegel variety. Its main idea is to interpret level-forgetting maps on suitable ordinary neighborhoods as lifts of relative Frobenius.

1. [[perfectoid-siegel-variety/anticanonical-tower/siegel-modular-varieties|Siegel Modular Varieties and Level Structures]] — the moduli problem, $K^p$-orbits, $p$-levels, formal generic fibres and the good-reduction locus (including the affine/projective-line examples).
